# 7. AI tools and academic integrity

## Allowed

You may use AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini and similar) **to learn**: asking for explanations and
hints, and getting help understanding an error.

## Not allowed

- AI assistants and internet search in **weekly tests, phase tests and the final exam**, unless the paper explicitly
  allows them.
- Submitting work you cannot explain, whether it came from AI, the internet or a classmate.
- Entering anyone's personal data into an external AI tool.

## The rule for projects

**You must be able to explain and change any line you submit.** Tutors run short viva checks: they may point at a
line and ask what it does, why it is there, or how you would change it.

For the capstone, AI coding assistants may be used under the same rule: every team member must be able to explain and
modify their own code during the viva.

## What happens if work is copied

Copied work (from classmates, the internet, or AI without understanding) gets **zero for that item** and one re-attempt
at a capped score. Repeated cases go to the program coordinator.
