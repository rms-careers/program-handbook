# Step 6: Submit your work

[← Step 5](05-save-and-upload-your-work.md) · **Step 6 of 7** · [Next: Other projects and team work →](07-other-projects-and-team-work.md)

Pushing to GitHub **does not** tell your tutor anything. You submit each project in the **RMS student portal** by
pasting your repository link.

## 1. Check your work is really on GitHub

Before submitting:
- [ ] Every file you want reviewed appears on `https://github.com/YOUR-USERNAME/rms-weekly-labs`.
- [ ] The week's README checklist is complete (for example `week-01/README.md`).
- [ ] `git status` on your computer says **nothing to commit, working tree clean** and
      **Your branch is up to date with 'origin/main'**. If it doesn't, you have work that is not on GitHub yet.
- [ ] The repo opens when you are **not** signed in. Test it by opening the link in a private/incognito browser window.

## 2. Copy the right link

Copy the link to the **repository itself**:

```text
✅ https://github.com/priya-sharma/rms-weekly-labs
❌ https://github.com/priya-sharma/rms-weekly-labs/tree/main/week-01        (a folder)
❌ https://github.com/priya-sharma/rms-weekly-labs/blob/main/README.md      (a file)
❌ https://github.com/priya-sharma                                          (your profile)
❌ https://github.com/rms-careers/template-weekly-labs                      (the template, not your copy)
```

The portal only accepts the `https://github.com/username/repository` form. Even though this repo holds many weeks,
you submit the same link every week; the brief tells your tutor which folder to look at.

## 3. Submit in the portal

1. Sign in at [student.rms-careers.com](https://student.rms-careers.com).
2. Open **Batch Workspace** from the menu and choose your batch.
3. Find the current week and open its project (for Week 1, the Weekly Build "Hello, Git").
4. Read the brief and the rubric once more.
5. Fill in the form:

   | Field | What to enter |
   |---|---|
   | **GitHub Repository URL \*** | Your repository link (required) |
   | **Live Demo URL (Optional)** | Only for deployed projects, from Week 9 onward |
   | **Notes for Reviewer (Optional)** | What you built, anything incomplete, and for team work your features and pull request links |

6. Click **Submit Repository**.

The project status changes to **Submitted for Review**.

## 4. What happens next

| Status | What it means | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **Submitted for Review** | Waiting for your tutor | You can still push fixes and click **Update Submission** until your tutor starts the review |
| **Under Tutor Review** | Your tutor is reviewing it | Wait; don't rewrite your commits |
| **Changes Requested** | Your tutor wants changes | Read the feedback, fix your work, commit, push, then submit again |
| **Project Approved** | Done | Read the feedback; it often has points worth mentioning in interviews |

Approved projects earn XP for your batch leaderboard.

## Due dates

Each project shows an open date and a due date. Weekly Builds are normally due on **Day 4 of the week at 23:59**.
Submitting a working, partly complete project on time is better than a perfect one that is late. Say what is missing in
**Notes for Reviewer**.
