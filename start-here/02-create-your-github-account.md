# Step 2: Create your GitHub account

> **Already have a GitHub account?** Skip to [Choose a professional username](#2-choose-a-professional-username) to
> check your username, then do [Secure your account](#4-secure-your-account) and
> [Complete your profile](#5-complete-your-profile).

## 1. Use an email you will keep

Use a **personal** email address that you will still have after college, for example a Gmail address. Your GitHub
account and portfolio should outlive your college email.

## 2. Choose a professional username

Your username appears in every project link, for example `https://github.com/priya-sharma/rms-weekly-labs`. It will be
on your resume.

| Good | Avoid |
|---|---|
| `priya-sharma` | `cool_coder_2005` |
| `psharma-dev` | `hacker-priya` |
| `priyasharma21` | your college roll number |

Use your real name or something close to it. Letters, numbers and single hyphens are allowed.

## 3. Sign up

1. Go to [github.com/signup](https://github.com/signup).
2. Enter your **email**, a strong **password** (at least 15 characters, or 8 with a number and a lowercase letter) and
   your **username**.
3. Complete the verification puzzle.
4. GitHub emails you a **launch code**. Type it in to verify your email.
5. If GitHub asks you to choose a plan, choose **Free**. You never need to pay for anything in this program.
6. You can skip the personalisation questions.

You now have a GitHub account. Your profile page is `https://github.com/YOUR-USERNAME`.

## 4. Secure your account

Your account will hold your whole portfolio, so protect it with **two-factor authentication (2FA)**. With 2FA, signing
in needs your password **and** a code from your phone.

1. Install an authenticator app on your phone, for example Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator.
2. On GitHub, click your profile picture (top right) → **Settings** → **Password and authentication**.
3. Click **Enable two-factor authentication** and follow the steps. You will scan a QR code with the app.
4. GitHub shows **recovery codes**. **Download them and keep them somewhere safe** (not only on your phone). If you lose
   your phone, these codes are the only way back into your account.

## 5. Complete your profile

Click your profile picture → **Settings** → **Public profile**, then add:
- **Name:** your full name.
- **Profile picture:** a clear photo of your face (it does not need to be formal).
- **Bio:** one line, for example "B.Tech CSE student · learning Python and web development".

Click **Update profile**.

## 6. Add your GitHub link to the RMS student portal

1. Sign in at [student.rms-careers.com](https://student.rms-careers.com).
2. Open **Profile** from the menu.
3. In **Career & Placement Profile**, fill in **GitHub Profile** with `https://github.com/YOUR-USERNAME`.
4. Click **Save Profile Changes**.

This lets your tutor find your work.

## Checklist

- [ ] I can sign in to github.com.
- [ ] My username is professional.
- [ ] Two-factor authentication is on, and my recovery codes are saved.
- [ ] My name and photo are on my GitHub profile.
- [ ] My GitHub link is saved in my RMS student profile.
